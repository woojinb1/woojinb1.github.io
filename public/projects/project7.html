Retro-Forge: A Multi-Step Pairwise Retrosynthesis Framework for Solid-State Materials Synthesis

ICML 2026 Workshop Paper

Project Overview

AI-driven materials discovery can generate promising structures at scale, but those structures remain theoretical without valid synthesis routes. Retro-Forge addresses this gap by formulating precursor prediction as a sequence of learnable pairwise reactions rather than a single-step prediction problem.

The framework is built on a chemically valid Pairwise Reaction Dataset containing approximately 6,000 entries. A pairwise reactant prediction model is trained with synthetic data augmentation and recursively composed through tree search to produce complete, multi-step synthesis routes for solid-state materials. Under a contamination-free evaluation protocol, Retro-Forge matches state-of-the-art precursor-prediction baselines.
